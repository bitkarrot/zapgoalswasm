About ZapGoals

Build a shareable goal, track incoming sats live and let supporters pay with any Lightning wallet.

Each goal exposes a direct LNURL-pay endpoint for compatible wallets.
Lightning Address routing and NIP-57 receipt signing are not exposed by the current WASM host.
